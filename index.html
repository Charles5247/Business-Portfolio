About Supremework Synergy

Two Decades of Indigenous Engineering Excellence

Supremework Synergy Nig. Ltd operating under its Electro-Thermodynamics Engineering Division has been active in the field since 2006 and was formally incorporated on 22 June 2010 under RC 894327. From our head office in Sabon Gari, Kano, we deliver steam, electrical and fabrication engineering solutions to manufacturing, agricultural, construction and processing companies across Nigeria.

Established 2006 — two decades of hands-on field experience in electro-thermodynamic engineering.
Incorporated 22 June 2010 under RC 894327 with the Corporate Affairs Commission.
Head Office No. 19 Burma Road, Sabon Gari, Kano.

What We Do

Full-Scope Electro-Thermodynamic Engineering

Seven core service lines covering steam systems, power, rotating equipment, fabrication and site infrastructure, backed by direct field maintenance capability.

01

Steam Boiler Installation & Maintenance

Installation, repair, refurbishing and re-tubing of steam boilers, plus control panel work. We also run retainership maintenance agreements to keep boiler plants running reliably year-round.

02

Steam Management Systems

Steam line lagging and insulation, condensate return systems, steam load control and steam trapping engineered to cut energy loss and improve plant efficiency.

03

Electrical Installation & Repairs

Power installations from transformer to distribution point, industrial control and automated electrical design, and panel troubleshooting for manufacturing and processing plants.

04

Rewinding of Motors & Alternators

Full rewinding service for electric motors and alternators of any capacity or size, restoring rotating equipment to reliable working condition.

05

Metal Fabrication

Storage tanks, overhead and service tanks, feed mill machines, mixers, hammer mills and silos fabricated to specification for industrial and agro-processing operations.

06

Asphalt Plant Services

Installation, repair and maintenance of asphalt plants covering both the mechanical and electrical sides of plant operation.

07

Spare Parts Supply

Supply of genuine and compatible spare parts for boilers, electrical systems, motors, fabrication equipment and asphalt plants keeping your machines running.
